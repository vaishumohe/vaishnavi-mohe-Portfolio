About Me

Who am I, really?

I'm Vaishnavi — a backend-focused developer and UI/UX designer from Mumbai who likes building the part of a product people never see but always feel: the API, the database, the auth layer, the model behind the prediction. I'm completing my B.E. in Artificial Intelligence & Data Science at N.Y.S.S. Datta Meghe College of Engineering, Airoli, after a Diploma in Computer Science & Engineering from Siddhivinayak Technical Campus, Shegaon. My core stack is Python, FastAPI and SQL.

I've shipped a full AI health platform (ArogyaAI) with a FastAPI backend, an EfficientNetB0 image classifier, Google Gemini integration, Firebase auth and Docker deployment — delivered with a React + TypeScript front end by a 4-member team. I've also built an end-to-end voice analytics pipeline with Whisper AI, BERT and Power BI.

I care about request validation, clean database integration and products that actually work in the real world — and I design the screens too, using Figma for wireframes, user flows and responsive UI, so the product feels as considered as it functions. I'm looking for a backend, full-stack or UI/UX role where I can contribute and grow fast.
